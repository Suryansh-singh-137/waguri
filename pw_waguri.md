# Physics Wallah — Postmortem

## What They Were
Physics Wallah was an ed-tech startup that provided educational content and services to students, with a mission to make education hyper-affordable and democratized. Founded in 2016 as a YouTube channel by Alakh Pandey, the company quickly gained popularity and expanded its services to include teaching physics and chemistry for various exams. With over 46 million students and 4 million paid subscribers, Physics Wallah was one of the most promising ed-tech startups in India.

## The Timeline
The key events in Physics Wallah's history include its founding as a YouTube channel in 2016, the establishment of the company in 2020, and the raising of $100 million in a Series A funding round in June 2022, followed by a $210 million Series B funding round later in 2022. These funding rounds valued the company at $1.1 billion and $2.8 billion, respectively.

## The Debate
### The Case Against (Critic)
The critic argues that Physics Wallah's core fatal flaw was overexpansion and scaling too quickly, without adequately addressing core customer needs and ensuring operational efficiency. This flaw was exacerbated by the company's rapid growth and massive funding, which created unrealistic expectations and pressures to scale. The critic also points to secondary causes, such as intense competition in the ed-tech space, failure to address core customer needs, and inadequate operational efficiency, as contributing factors to the company's failure.

### The Case For (Devil's Advocate)
The devil's advocate argues that Physics Wallah's failure was not inevitable and that the company had several key strengths, including strong brand recognition, a unique teaching style, and a mission to make education hyper-affordable and democratized. The devil's advocate suggests that Physics Wallah could have addressed potential challenges by focusing on core customer needs, diversifying revenue streams, and investing in technology and infrastructure. The devil's advocate also points to external factors, such as intense competition and regulatory challenges, as contributing to the company's demise.

### The Verdict
In conclusion, while Physics Wallah had several key strengths, its rapid expansion and scaling ultimately led to its downfall. The company's failure to address core customer needs and ensure operational efficiency, combined with intense competition and external factors, made it difficult for the company to sustain its growth and valuation. However, it is also possible that with a more focused approach to customer needs and a more strategic expansion, Physics Wallah could have avoided failure and achieved long-term success.

## Cause of Death
The ranked list of causes of Physics Wallah's death is as follows:
1. **Overexpansion and scaling too quickly**: The company's rapid growth and expansion outpaced its ability to establish efficient operational systems, leading to financial and operational challenges.
2. **Failure to address core customer needs**: Physics Wallah's focus on scaling and expansion may have led to a neglect of core customer needs, resulting in a mismatch between the company's offerings and customer expectations.
3. **Intense competition in the ed-tech space**: The company faced significant competition from other ed-tech startups, making it difficult to differentiate itself and maintain market share.
4. **Inadequate operational efficiency**: The company's rapid growth and expansion may have outpaced its ability to establish efficient operational systems, leading to financial and operational challenges.

## Lessons for Founders
The lessons for founders from Physics Wallah's postmortem are:
1. **Focus on core customer needs**: Ensure that your company's offerings are aligned with customer expectations and needs, and prioritize customer satisfaction above rapid expansion.
2. **Diversify revenue streams**: Develop a mix of revenue streams to reduce dependence on a single source of income and increase financial stability.
3. **Invest in technology and infrastructure**: Allocate resources to develop a robust and scalable technology infrastructure to support growth and improve the overall customer experience.
4. **Prioritize strategic expansion**: Focus on strategic expansion into new markets and prioritize partnerships and collaborations to drive growth, rather than rapid scaling.
5. **Maintain operational efficiency**: Establish efficient operational systems and prioritize operational efficiency to ensure that growth is sustainable and profitable.
